Most drivers take a hairpin like a U: brake into the apex, slowest at the apex, throttle after it.

The fast way is a V. Brake in a straight line, let the kart rotate on the outside, and be flat out before you reach the apex. The apex is where you are already accelerating, not where you are slowest.

Save it for your next session 👇
