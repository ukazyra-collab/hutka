Same kerb, same moment. In F1 it is the fast way through the chicane. In a kart it is a mistake.

An F1 car has suspension and downforce, so the kerb just straightens the line. A kart has no suspension: the kerb throws it in the air, the tyres stop gripping and you land slower than you took off.
