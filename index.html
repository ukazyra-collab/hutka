    Import tracks from OpenStreetMap

    OpenStreetMap knows most karting tracks in the world (name, position, often the website). Pick a country, tick the tracks worth having, import — they become catalogue pages that drivers find by GPS when they upload.

    Layouts

    Layout

    Shown on the track page and in the upload window so drivers pick the right configuration.
    Open layout page

    1Track line

    2Corner numbering

    Corners are found automatically from the radius threshold. With “Edit corners” on, click the line to add a corner, right-click a number to remove it.

    3Satellite imagery

    GPS and imagery can be a few metres apart. With “Align imagery” on, drag the picture under the line (or use the arrows, 0.5 m a step) until the track matches, then save.
